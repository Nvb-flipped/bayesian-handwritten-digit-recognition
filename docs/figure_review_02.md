# Per-figure scientific review

Reviewed on 8 October 2026. The generator is [figures.py](../src/bayes_digits/figures.py); [manifest](../results/figure_manifest.json) binds source/generator hashes, dimensions and 300-DPI PNG settings. Every figure has PDF/SVG/PNG versions. [Transformed data](../results/figure_data.json) and [scalar CSV](../results/scalar_metrics.csv) supplement the visual displays.

| Figure / scientific question | Exact evidence and transformation | Visual/uncertainty review and bounded conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Dataset: are splits balanced and what are the inputs? | splits.npz, dataset.json, config.json; bundled load_digits images. Counts by class 0–9; first indexed training example of each class. | Zero-based count axis; split sizes and hatch/color legend below panels; fixed grayscale 0–1 and nearest-neighbor pixels. Nearly balanced counts; images are illustrative training inputs, not successful test cases. |
| Inference: where do fitting, selection and prediction occur? | config.json plus generator logic: 64 pixels, 32 features, 330 head parameters, 512 samples. | Numbered snake sequence with arrows and explicit validation/test roles; readable at report width. A computational diagram, not measured performance evidence. |
| Comparison: how do recognition and probability scores differ? | metrics.json, summary.json. Error count = total confusion support minus trace; NLL natural-log nats; Brier summed over ten classes. | Zero-based axes, three offset seed shapes, black mean ± sample SD, NB singleton diamond. All methods use the same 360 images. Neural methods beat NB recognition; MAP beats Laplace proper scores. Seed SD is not test-set CI. |
| Classes: which labels are difficult? | metrics.json. Normalize each seed's confusion row by true-class support, then average; F1 deficit = 1 − F1. Raw seed F1 is saved. | Class order 0–9; sequential confusion scale fixed 0–1, annotations are rounded integer percentages for proportions above 0.005. Every seed is plotted as a shape, with filled MAP/open Laplace markers, black mean dashes and observed min–max spans. Axis starts at zero; no SD/CI bars are truncated or implied. Digit 8 is weakest; raw F1/support table accompanies the panel. |
| Calibration: is confidence reliable and does disagreement separate errors? | metrics.json and predictions.npz, prespecified seed 11. Ten equal-width confidence bins; equal-size points and an aligned 3×10 count table, including empty bins; MI ECDF separately within correct/error groups. | Unconnected reliability points, identity line and method shapes; axes are exactly 0–1; missing bins are omitted from points and retained as zero counts. MI uses nats; ECDFs state 352/eight counts and use solid/dashed lines. Underconfidence and overlapping disagreement distributions limit detection claims. |
| Convergence: how did feature losses change? | history_11/22/33.json; both train and validation losses evaluated after each optimizer update. | Shared positive NLL limits 0.025–3 on base-10 log axes; epochs linear from 0 to 120; seed shapes/styles and legend. Curves improve over the fixed budget, not proof of asymptotic convergence; no posterior optimization trajectory is implied. |
| Sensitivity: what prior wins validation and what do frozen-prior controls show? | sensitivity.json and metrics.json. Prior grid 0.1/1/10; test NLL of matched MAP/full/diagonal precision at the locked LA prior. | Left log10 prior axis with styled seed guides between discrete candidates, zero-based NLL. Right offset seed shapes and sample SD; method colors consistent. Alpha 1 wins this grid; full beats diagonal but loses to matched MAP. Test scores do not choose alpha. |
| Monte Carlo: can finite draws alone explain the NLL penalty? | review_diagnostics/summary.json and matched_map.json; raw probabilities/records and configuration in the same directory. Three fixed feature models × three counts × eight repetitions. | Log2 sample-count axis with explicit 128/512/2048 ticks; NLL penalty and repetition SD from zero. Error bars are sampling-repeat SD conditional on model/test set, not seed/test CI. Penalty persists at 2048 draws while integration SD declines. |
| Covariance geometry: why can dropping correlation increase contrast variance? | Exact two-label scalar-feature Hessian from laplace.py: full covariance [[0.75,0.25],[0.25,0.75]], diagonal-precision covariance (2/3)I. | Equal-scale radius-one Mahalanobis contours and exact Gaussian logit-contrast densities, variances 1 and 4/3. Explicitly labeled toy, not measured ten-class evidence or 68% joint regions; contours verified numerically. |
| Errors: what mistakes does the prespecified model make? | predictions.npz indices/probabilities, original load_digits pixels. All eight seed-11 errors sorted by original index. | Fixed grayscale 0–1, native 8×8 nearest-neighbor display, titles with index/true→predicted/confidence. No smoothing or cherry-picked favorable examples; ambiguity is a descriptive interpretation. |

All ten individual exports were visually checked; their embedded versions occur on final report pages 5, 6, 8 and 10–14, checked as part of inspection of all 15 pages. The extra unnumbered diagram is the mathematically tested toy covariance example, not new measured results. Legends avoid data occlusion. Font/annotation sizes were enlarged where needed; vector analytical content is retained in the PDF. The small raster digit panels intentionally preserve measured pixel structure.

Method colors: NB blue, MAP orange, Laplace green, diagonal variant purple. Seed colors in within-method panels encode a different, explicitly labeled role and also use circle/square/triangle and solid/dashed/dotted styles. Color is not the only distinction. Numerical SD is descriptive; no p-value, significance or population confidence interval is inferred from three shared-test seeds.
